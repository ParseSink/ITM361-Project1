Welcome To My Page

Hello, I'm Joel!

Currently I am a junior at Illinois Institute of Technology. My focus is in Information Technology Management (ITM). I am hoping to become a System Administrator after I graduate. This has been a really big passion of mine for several years. I started with using an old library computer to host a few game servers for me and my friends and over the years it has developed into a full-blown server rack.

This passion and hobby of mine has developed into many fun skills and knowledge. I always like finding something new to use my servers; I have had the chance to explore everything from networking to setting up and running local AI agents or using Raspberry Pis to setup clusters.

My Hobbies

  • Drone Racing and Building: First Person View (FPV) drone racing is something I have been doing with one of my friends for a few years. It started when they asked me to spot for them to keep them safe. After a few sessions I was hooked and bought my first drone, a whoop, a small form factor drone. This one is a great fit for anyone trying to learn how to fly fpv drones. A whoop drone is designed for indoor use and requires no additional training for certifications from the FAA. This is still my favorite form factor of drones especially for flying indoors.

  • 3D Modeling and Printing: I love to explore new technologies. When I first heard about 3D printing, I learned to design objects using modeling and slicing software. My first Printer was an Ender 3. This one worked well for me for years and I learnd a lot of skills and information on how FDM printing works. I recently decided to upgrade to a Bambu P2S. THis allowed for me to print even more detailed modles and smaller parts its come in handy for a quick fix in many situations or when I have a create idea I want to put together. creating brackets to mount hardware to a server rack or using it for replacement parts Its been one of my favorite tools.

  • HomeLab and Networking: As mentioned earlier, a big part of my life is dedicated to Networking and Server management. This diagram is what I currently manage daily for all of my server hosting needs. Proxmox is the backbone of my homelab, Its a hypervisor that allows me to run multiple services and programs on the same hardware in containers and virtual machines. Currently The main use of my homelab is for hosing private game servers, a SFTP server, DNS filtering and ad-blocking, and hosting local AI agents. I always am interesting in hosting and learning about new services.

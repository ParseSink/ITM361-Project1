The Underground City of Hope

The year is 2180, The world has been ravaged by war, fammine and disease. The ultra elite have taken refue off planet. The rest of humanity have been left to fend for themselves. 20 years prior, The members of the off planet elite reprogramed the once harmless recource gathering robots called Arc. The Arc became highly agressive in an attempt to cull the remainder of humanity. They exist to rid the earth dwellers to maxamize the output of the planet for the of world elite. When met with highly agressive robots humanity fled underground into the toledo network. Speranza, the headquartes of the toledo network, is just one of many hidden cities offereing safety and refuge to those in need. This network spans across modern day itally. You as a Raider are tasked with a dangerous task... going topside.
